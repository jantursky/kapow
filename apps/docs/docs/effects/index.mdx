Every effect is a factory on one class. Change effects are `ChangeEffect.<name>()`, conditional effects `ConditionalEffect.<name>()`, progress effects `ProgressEffect.<name>()`, and transitions `KapowEnter.<name>()` or `KapowExit.<name>()`. The [guides](/guides/change-effects) explain how each kind is attached. The [live demo]({{demo}}) shows all of them running.

Particle and movement effects fall back to a short fade with [reduced motion](/guides/reduced-motion).

## Change effects

Attach with `Modifier.changeEffect(value, effect)`. They play once each time `value` changes.

| Effect | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `shine` | A soft band of light sweeps across the element's drawn pixels. |
| `pulse` | Rings or fills in the element's shape grow outward behind it and fade. |
| `glow` | A colored halo flares up and fades. Quick repeats make it stronger, up to a limit. |
| `spray` | A burst of particles fans upward from the element, grows, then fades. |
| `rise` | One particle per change, such as "+1", floats up from the element and fades. |
| `spin` | The element turns one or more full turns and settles where it started. |

## Conditional effects

Attach with `Modifier.conditionalEffect(condition, effect)`. They stay on while `condition` is true.

| Effect | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `repeat` | Plays a change effect when the condition turns true, then every interval while it stays true. |
| `glow` | A steady halo in the element's shape that fades in and out with the condition. |
| `pushDown` | The element scales down slightly, as if pressed, and springs back. |
| `smoke` | Soft particles rise from the top of the element. |

## Progress effects

Attach with `Modifier.progressEffect(effect) { progress }`. You control the progress from 0 to 1.

| Effect | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `shine` | A band of light whose position across the element follows the progress. |
| `glow` | A halo whose strength follows the progress. |
| `iris` | A circular reveal from an origin whose radius follows the progress. |

## Transitions

Attach with `Modifier.kapowTransition(scope, enter, exit)` inside `AnimatedVisibility` or `AnimatedContent`, or use `KapowAnimatedVisibility`.

| Transition | Enter | Exit | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `iris` | yes | yes | A circle grows from an origin until it covers the content, or shrinks back. |
| `clock` | yes | yes | A sweep around an origin, clockwise from 12 o'clock, like a clock hand. |
| `wipe` | yes | yes | A straight edge moves across the content at an angle. |
| `glare` | yes | yes | A wipe with a bright streak along its edge. |
| `blinds` | yes | yes | Slats open or close like venetian blinds, optionally staggered. |
| `pop` | yes | no | The content scales in with an overshoot while a ring and a burst of particles go off behind it. |
| `anvil` | yes | no | The content drops in from above, lands with a shake and kicks up dust. |
| `poof` | no | yes | The content vanishes at once in a puff of smoke. |
